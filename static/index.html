DATA SCOPE

What is in this dashboard?

The core monthly series is RBI DBIE Table 16, industry-wise deployment of bank credit. Table 15 is a separate major-sector series used for broader context; it is not added into Table 16 totals.

CreditLens describes aggregate scheduled commercial bank lending by sector. It does not measure borrower defaults or individual credit risk.

Methodology & Source Governance Notes

Primary Source: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Database on Indian Economy (DBIE), Table 16 (Industry-wise Deployment of Bank Credit) and Table 15 (Major Sectors).

Aggregate Data vs Loan-Level Default Risk: The RBI sectoral deployment dataset captures macro-level aggregate credit outstanding deployed by scheduled commercial banks. It is not an individual borrower dataset and does not contain credit scores, probability of default (PD), or realized credit losses.

Break A (January 2019 Format Revision): Sectoral credit data are based on a revised format effective January 2019; component series published earlier underwent classification adjustments. CreditLens maintains a continuous series from Jan 2019 to Jun 2026.

Break B (July 28, 2023 HDFC Merger): Credit data from July 28, 2023 onward incorporates the non-bank merger of HDFC Ltd. into HDFC Bank, introducing a structural level shift of over ₹3.5 Lakh Cr into non-food bank credit.
